Three Intermarried Extended Families (7 Generations)

The Hartwell, Navarro, and Okafor families each span seven generations. Their lines intermarry across all three houses (Hartwell↔Navarro, Navarro↔Okafor, and Okafor↔Hartwell), while the timeline is staged so only five generations overlap contemporaneously (G3–G7). This sample includes 46 total people with at least 12 members represented in each family.

Mobile-first data entry

Photos are stored in-browser as data URLs for this demo and are embedded in the chart data as avatar.
